SMS Consent

DESCODE LLC sends authorized contractors and registered JobOps users transactional work-order notifications only.

SMS Consent

By providing your mobile phone number and selecting the SMS consent checkbox, you agree to receive recurring transactional SMS messages from DESCODE LLC through JobOps. Messages may include account invitations, work-order assignments, scheduling information, work-order changes, status updates, completion notifications, and related service communications. Message frequency varies based on work-order activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

How Opt-In Works

  • Contractors enter their mobile number while registering or activating a JobOps account.
  • The SMS checkbox is optional, separate, and unchecked by default.
  • Users must affirmatively select it to provide consent.
  • Consent status and timestamp may be recorded.
  • Users can withdraw consent by replying STOP.
  • Users can request assistance by replying HELP.

Types of Messages

  • New work-order assignments
  • Scheduling information and changes
  • Work-order status updates
  • Work-order completion notifications
  • Account invitations and service-related notifications

No Marketing

JobOps SMS messages are transactional and operational. DESCODE LLC does not use this SMS program for promotional or third-party marketing messages.

Mobile Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
